Phase II — Cryptographic Integrity & Chain-of-Custody (ISO/IEC 27037)

Dual Hashing & Merkle Custody Ledger (custody/ledger.py)

Simultaneous computation of SHA-256 and BLAKE3 digests before parsing. Entries sealed into immutable chain_of_custody.jsonl with atomic os.fsync durability and automated tampering detection.

🔒 Is Data Encrypted / Protected?
🔑 [YES] Protected-data Analysis (crypto/protected_data.py)
  • Identify encryption type: AES-128/256-CBC, XOR Scramble, Password Zip, Shannon entropy audit
  • Find keys / access method: Auto vendor masks (DJI 0x77, Autel), firmware keystores, investigator passphrase
  • Use authorized decryption: In-memory or verified derivative working copy
  • Preserve encrypted data: Dual-hash raw ciphertext; record PROTECTED_DATA_DECRYPT in Merkle ledger
📄 [NO] Direct Extraction
  • Extract data directly: Open autopilot logs (PX4, ArduPilot, Parrot, MAVLink, Betaflight, Yuneec, EXIF)
  • Verify integrity: Confirm unadulterated bitstream against acquisition ledger
  • Proceed to parsing: Feed stream directly to multi-vendor deserializer
