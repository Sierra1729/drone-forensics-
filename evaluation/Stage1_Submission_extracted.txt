1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
1.1 Solution Vision & Legal Standards Compliance 
Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) have evolved into critical autonomous infrastructure across 
commercial, civil, and military operations. However, their widespread availability has introduced 
grave security vulnerabilities, including unauthorized airspace incursions, critical infrastructure 
surveillance, trans-border contraband delivery, and cyber-physical interference. In forensic 
investigations and judicial proceedings, the integrity, mathematical repeatability, and physical 
provenance of extracted UAV flight data represent the foundational pillars of courtroom 
admissibility.

To address this mandate under the PUSHPAK Grand Challenge 2026, Team Halos presents Garud 
FX: Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Forensic Toolkit—an indigenous, multi-vendor, hardware-
integrated forensic software suite engineered for end-to-end UAV investigation. Garud FX strictly 
adheres to international digital evidence standards ISO/IEC 27037:2012 (Identification, collection, 
acquisition, and preservation of digital evidence) and NIST SP 800-86 (Guide to Integrating 
Forensic Techniques into Incident Response). Furthermore, Garud FX natively incorporates 
statutory compliance workflows for Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 
2023 (replacing Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872). Specifically, Garud FX auto-
populates a Section 63(4)-compliant digital evidence certificate template, pre-filled with dual 
cryptographic hash manifests, binary offsets, and hardware device signatures, ready for statutory 
execution by the designated device custodian and independent expert examiner. 
1.2 Key Architectural Innovations 
• 
Hex-Offset Provenance Mapping: Every extracted telemetry event (geodetic coordinates, IMU dynamics, 
battery voltage, flight mode) maintains an immutable, bi-directional link to its exact raw hexadecimal byte 
offset within the underlying binary evidence dump (e.g., Offset 0x0001F820). Forensic examiners can 
scrub between 3D spatial visualization and raw hex inspector view for zero-trust court verification. 
• 
Air-Gapped 3D Offline Trajectory Reconstruction: Garud FX embeds a self-contained local MBTiles 
raster/vector mapping server and an offline 3D spatial trajectory engine. Examiners perform complete 3D 
flight path analysis, altitude profiling, and camera orientation visualization inside air-gapped cleanrooms 
without internet connectivity. 
• 
Signature-Matching & Fallback Binary Carving: UAV crash incidents frequently trigger sudden power 
disconnects, corrupting log files missing end-of-file (EOF) structures. Garud FX pattern-matches raw byte 
streams against 9 pre-registered multi-vendor format signatures with a fallback binary carving engine to 
recover intact telemetry up to the microsecond of power loss. 
• 
In-Stream Dual Cryptographic Hashing & SQLite Case Packaging: During the initial hardware read 
pass, Garud FX computes simultaneous SHA-256 and BLAKE3 cryptographic hashes without secondary 
I/O overhead. All custody actions are recorded in an append-only hash-chained custody ledger 
(custody.ledger) and packaged into a single, queryable SQLite forensic database (.garud_db format).

2. UNDERSTANDING OF THE PROBLEM 
2.1 Technical Challenges in Drone Digital Forensics 
• 
Volatile RAM Buffers & Abrupt Crash Disconnects: Flight controllers buffer high-frequency telemetry in 
volatile SRAM. Mid-air collisions or battery ejections cut main power rails instantly, preventing clean OS 
unmounts or EOF marker writes. Standard parsers fail on such truncated binary files. 
• 
Multi-Vendor Proprietary Encrypted & Obfuscated Schemas: UAV manufacturers employ closed binary 
formats. DJI utilizes XOR obfuscation and AES-128 payload encryption across DAT file versions (Fly0182, 
P3/P4, Mavic 3). Open-source platforms (PX4, ArduPilot) utilize dynamic binary structure packing requiring 
runtime schema extraction. 
• 
OS-Level Metadata Contamination: Connecting recovered UAV media directly to host workstations 
(Windows/macOS) risks immediate evidence spoliation due to automated hidden system file writes 
(.DS_Store, System Volume Info) and modified access timestamps. 
• 
Timezone Skew & Multi-Clock Drift: Drone investigations involve multiple time domains: UTC 
timestamps from onboard GPS receivers, microsecond epoch counters from flight controller clocks, and 
local time settings on operator mobile GCS devices. 
• 
Air-Gap Cleanroom Requirements: Secure forensic cleanrooms operate under complete network 
isolation. Traditional forensic software relying on online mapping APIs (Google Maps, Mapbox) fail in air-
gapped environments. 
2.2 Target Forensic Investigation Scenarios 
• 
Airspace Intrusion & Critical Infrastructure Defense: Reconstructing flight paths of rogue UAVs over 
restricted airspace, airports, or military installations to pinpoint launch coordinates and operator positioning. 
• 
Contraband Delivery & Border Smuggling: Analyzing recovered crash or intercepted drones used for 
smuggling narcotics or contraband across international borders or penal perimeters. 
• 
Mid-Air Collision & Aircraft Incident Reconstruction: Investigating aerial collisions with commercial 
aircraft or infrastructure to determine pilot inputs, battery collapse, or mechanical failure. 
• 
GPS Spoofing & Cyber-Physical Attack Verification: Detecting electronic warfare interference, GPS 
spoofing, or command injection targeted at autonomous drone platforms.

3. PROPOSED DRONE FORENSIC TOOLKIT 
3.1 Garud FX System Architecture 
Garud FX is constructed around a 5-layer modular software architecture designed for high 
scalability, air-gapped security, and complete vendor independence: 
• Layer 1 (Acquisition Layer): Interfaces with raw physical media (.dd eMMC/SD), Android GCS 
devices via ADB, and live serial USB MAVLink hardware ports using non-destructive streaming. 
• Layer 2 (Preservation & Custody Layer): Computes simultaneous SHA-256 and BLAKE3 hashes, 
maintains an append-only hash-chained custody ledger (custody.ledger), compiles artifacts into an 
indexed SQLite database (.garud_db format via GarudCasePackager), and manages Section 63(4) 
BSA certificate templates. 
• Layer 3 (Parsing & Carving Layer): Houses 9 multi-vendor binary parsers (PX4 ULog, ArduPilot 
DataFlash, DJI DAT, DJI CSV, Autel, Yuneec, Betaflight, MAVLink, EXIF/SRT) equipped with pattern-
matching carving fallback engines. 
• Layer 4 (Analytics & Correlation Engine): Executes spatial-temporal synchronization, Haversine 
metrics, geofence ray-casting, velocity anomaly detection, and GPS spoofing verification. 
• Layer 5 (Presentation & Reporting Layer): Delivers an air-gapped Web GUI (Leaflet 2D, Cesium 3D 
globe, 3-column raw hex inspector linked to .garud_db queries) and an automated PDF courtroom 
exhibit generator.

3.3 Digital Evidence Acquisition Methodology 
Garud FX mandates non-destructive physical and logical acquisition workflows conforming to 
ISO/IEC 27037 Clause 6.3: 
1. Chunked Read-Only Physical Imaging: Block-level chunked reads (64KB chunks) wrapped in 
read-only file streams (rb). Integrates with physical USB write-blockers and queries OS mount 
controllers to suppress write-backs. 
2. Hardware Serial MAVLink Micro-Chunk Extraction: Communicates over serial USB using 
MAVLink v2.0 protocol. Transmits LOG_REQUEST_LIST and fetches log binary data via 
LOG_REQUEST_DATA in 240-byte micro-chunks with 16-bit CRC checksum validation. 
3. Logical Android ADB GCS Extraction: Interfaces via adb to pull companion GCS application logs 
(DJI Fly, DJI Go 4, Autel Explorer) directly from mobile partitions. 
3.4 Evidence Preservation & Cryptographic Integrity 
Evidence integrity is maintained through in-stream dual cryptographic hashing (SHA-256 and 
BLAKE3) calculated during the initial raw byte read. Actions are logged in an append-only JSONL 
hash-chained custody ledger (custody.ledger) containing monotonic timestamps, action types, 
investigator signatures, and cryptographic parent-hash linkage. The complete case case is packaged 
into an indexed SQLite forensic database (.garud_db) for tamper-evident distribution. 
 
To satisfy Indian statutory requirements under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 
(BSA), 2023, Garud FX auto-populates a Section 63(4)-compliant digital evidence certificate 
template, pre-filled with SHA-256/BLAKE3 hash manifests, binary offsets, and hardware device 
signatures, ready for statutory execution by the designated device custodian and independent 
expert examiner.

3.5 Data Extraction & Signature-Matching Carving Engine 
To salvage truncated crash logs, Garud FX pattern-matches raw byte streams against 9 pre-
registered multi-vendor format signatures (0x55 0x4C 0x6F 0x67 ULog, 0xA3 0x95 DataFlash, 0x46 
0x4C 0x59 DJI DAT) with a fallback binary carving engine to unpack intact telemetry packets up to 
the corruption horizon. All data is normalized into a canonical time-series schema (timestamp_utc, 
lat, lon, alt_msl, roll, pitch, yaw, battery_v, flight_mode, raw_hex_offset). 
4. DATA ANALYSIS & AUTOMATED FORENSIC REPORTING 
4.1 Multi-Source Timeline Synchronization 
The analytical correlation engine (analytics/correlation.py) aligns disparate time-series evidence 
across onboard blackbox logs, mobile GCS databases, and controller logs. The module establishes 
UTC GPS reference anchors to calculate microsecond clock drift, interpolates missing packets, and 
merges multi-source telemetry into a synchronized event timeline. 
4.2 Kinematic Anomaly & Cyber Threat Detection 
• 
Stationary Hovering vs. Active Flight: Analyzes horizontal speed vectors (< 0.5 m/s over > 30s) to 
identify surveillance hovering over sensitive facilities. 
• 
GPS Spoofing & Spatial Teleportation: Flags physically impossible spatial jumps (> 50 m/s delta) or 
sudden HDOP degradation indicating electronic GPS spoofing. 
• 
Power Collapse & Free-Fall Verification: Correlates sudden battery voltage drops (< 10.5V) with high 
vertical acceleration spikes to pinpoint crash impact timestamps. 
• 
Geofence Breach Ray-Casting: Executes point-in-polygon ray-casting against air-gapped geospatial 
geofence polygons, flagging unauthorized airspace incursions. 
4.3 Raw Hex Offset Traceability & PDF Exhibit Generation 
Garud FX provides a 3-column raw hexadecimal inspector ([Offset (8 Hex)] | [16 Hex Bytes] | 
[ASCII]). Every parsed telemetry coordinate links directly to its raw byte offset (e.g. Offset 
0x0001F820). During courtroom cross-examination, examiners query the compiled .garud_db 
SQLite database container for instant spatial-temporal indexing and click any 2D/3D coordinate to 
jump directly to the exact raw byte offset in the binary dump. 
Complete cases are exported into courtroom-ready PDF exhibits containing Executive Summaries, 
Flight Path Maps, Anomaly Logs, Hash-Chained Custody Manifests, and pre-filled Section 63(4) BSA 
Certificates.

6. VALIDATION & TESTING METHODOLOGY 
6.1 Test Environment & Repeatability Framework 
Garud FX is validated within isolated, air-gapped forensic cleanrooms. The testing environment 
comprises Windows 11 Enterprise and Ubuntu 24.04 LTS workstations with hardware USB write-
blockers, FTDI serial adapters, and SITL emulators running PX4 and ArduPilot flight dynamics. 
Validation utilizes reference raw images, field test crash logs, and synthetic anomaly injections 
following adapted NIST Computer Forensic Tool Testing (CFTT) principles for UAV platforms. 
Repeatability is confirmed by executing independent ingest runs on identical evidence images and 
verifying 100% identical SHA-256 output digests. If any evidence file or ledger block exhibits 
tampering, Garud FX halts ingestion, raises visual tamper alerts, and flags the compromised node.
